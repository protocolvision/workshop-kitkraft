# Discovery interview — "Northside Housing Trust" (synthetic), 2026-09-08

Context: ~35 staff, community housing nonprofit. Speaker: operations director.

1. Q: How does your team use AI today?
2. A: Honestly, everyone has ChatGPT open. I use it every Monday to turn the weekend's tenant emails into a summary for the maintenance lead.
3. Q: Every Monday?
4. A: Every Monday, forty-odd emails. I paste them in with a prompt I've kept in a Word doc for a year.
5. Q: Does anyone else use that prompt?
6. A: Nobody's asked. It only works because I know which tenants escalate and which buildings have the leaky roofs — the model doesn't know that, I add it.
7. Q: Other recurring things?
8. A: Our grants person drafts funder updates quarterly; she uses ChatGPT too, her own way. And board minutes — I've tried, but it gets the motions wrong, so I stopped.
9. Q: Anything written down that a new hire could pick up?
10. A: No. If I left, the Monday summary would leave with me. That's what worries the director.
11. Q: Is there anything the team shares?
12. A: A Teams channel where people paste prompts they liked. Nobody goes back to it.
13. Q: What would you want fixed first?
14. A: The Monday thing. It's the one everyone downstream depends on and it lives in my head.
