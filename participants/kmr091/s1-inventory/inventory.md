## Kits I use
### 1. policy research kit
- What it is: Claude chat, working from public government documents, news pieces and company announcements relevant to my policy area
- The job it does: Helps me get up to speed on a regulation or policy topic and what's changed, and gives me a view on a development, for example whether a suggested course of action would work
- How often I use it: Daily
- What I adapted: I check what it says against my own knowledge and understanding, and I treat its views on whether a proposal will work as input to my thinking, not as an answer
- Who else uses it: Only me
- Where it lives: In my Claude chat history, with no saved prompt

## Kit to convert
Policy research kit. The policy and political intelligence I need moves quickly every day, and my process is useful but only in my head. I want to write it down so I get the most out of it, and so my team can draw on the same intel and have discussions grounded in what's happening in real time. It is specific to me and my team, so I am not sure how others will benefit; it is the kind of analysis you might pay a political analysis firm for, now available daily for the cost of a subscription.
