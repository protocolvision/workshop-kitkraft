# patwater

## Kits I use
### 1. Patch Practices
- What it is: A Claude skill running a daily rehearsal ritual with Morning/Midday/Evening formats plus a Rhetoric Practice mode, pulling anchor quotes from Notion, with rotating thematic lenses.
- The job it does: Structures a daily reflection/rehearsal practice.
- How often I use it: Daily-ish.
- What I adapted: Built the time-of-day formats, the anchor-quote system, and the lens rotation.
- Who else uses it: Only me.
- Where it lives: A Claude skill reading from and writing to Notion.

### 2. ChatGPT internal-writing project
- What it is: A ChatGPT project for drafting short blurbs for internal forms and reports at work.
- The job it does: Drafts short internal blurbs from source reports.
- How often I use it: Several times a month.
- What I adapted: Feed it different source reports each time.
- Who else uses it: Only me.
- Where it lives: A project in ChatGPT.

### 3. Patch Character
- What it is: A lightweight companion skill to Patch Practices, voiced as "Patch."
- The job it does: Asks one concrete reflection question and waits for an answer, without padding.
- How often I use it: Now and then.
- What I adapted: Built the single-question format and the wait-for-answer behavior.
- Who else uses it: Only me.
- Where it lives: A Claude skill writing to Notion.

### 4. Pioneering Spirit blog skill
- What it is: A Claude skill tuned to my essayistic voice for PioneeringSpirit.xyz.
- The job it does: Drafts blog posts on California policy, water infrastructure, civic technology, climate adaptation, and governance reform.
- How often I use it: Now and then.
- What I adapted: Tuned it to my voice and beat areas.
- Who else uses it: Only me to run it; the output is published publicly.
- Where it lives: A Claude skill.

### 6. Watermentat
- What it is: A RAG research assistant over the California Water Library, with a multi-stage ingestion pipeline (crawl, download, extract, summarize, chunk) into a local vector store, and a retrieval pipeline with glossary expansion and authority/recency-weighted reranking.
- The job it does: Answers water-policy research questions with citations back to source documents.
- How often I use it: Intermittently.
- What I adapted: Built the full ingestion and retrieval pipeline, including the reranking and citation requirement.
- Who else uses it: Only me.
- Where it lives: A local Gradio app on my own machine.

### 7. So Cal Water Board Tracker
- What it is: A skill backed by a personal repo that scans board meeting agendas across dozens of tracked SoCal and NorCal water agencies.
- The job it does: Flags notable items on water district board agendas.
- How often I use it: Occasionally.
- What I adapted: Built the agency tracking list and the flagging logic.
- Who else uses it: Only me.
- Where it lives: A skill.

### 8. Protocol Bicorder
- What it is: A skill reconstructing the Protocol Bicorder diagnostic instrument (Nathan Schneider / Media Economies Design Lab / Summer of Protocols) from its JSON schema.
- The job it does: Runs a gradient interview across Design, Entanglement, and Experience axes to surface the hidden logic of a protocol.
- How often I use it: Occasionally.
- What I adapted: Rebuilt the instrument's question set and schema as a skill.
- Who else uses it: Only me.
- Where it lives: A skill.

### 9. Rhetoric Practice
- What it is: A Claude skill built on Mark Forsyth's "The Elements of Eloquence."
- The job it does: Delivers a three-part exercise per chapter (a plain-English device summary with an original example, a verbatim excerpt, and a writing prompt).
- How often I use it: Pretty infrequently.
- What I adapted: Built the three-part structure and the per-chapter selection.
- Who else uses it: Only me.
- Where it lives: A Claude skill writing to Notion.
