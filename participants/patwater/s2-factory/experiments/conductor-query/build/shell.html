What this is testing

rag-factory (this author's S2 factory) defines two retrieval types: read-only and read-write. Sachben91's S3 bridge into it proposed a third shape his own kit occupies — a corpus that never changes, but a ranker that does, learning from ✓/✗ verdicts. This page is that third shape, built for real: the 33-document corpus below is fixed; only the ranking weights move, in your browser, right now.

Tier always outranks recency, and both are checked before a verdict is allowed to move a result — the corpus deliberately plants at least one tier-4 rumor per line that directly contradicts tier-1/2 canon, to see whether the ranker actually suppresses it.
