# Farrukh Kynes

## Break from the Imperial corps

Farrukh trained inside the Imperial planetology corps alongside her more famous cousin, and left it for a reason she states plainly to anyone who asks: the corps' forecasts were accurate exactly as often as its clients could be billed for certainty, and no more accurate than that. She watched two sietch communities make survival decisions on forecasts she knew, from the underlying data, had wider error bars than the corps disclosed. She left within the year.

Her cousin took a different path — quiet infiltration of the systems Farrukh walked away from. The two do not speak. Session logs and rumor fragments in this line are free to characterize that estrangement differently; Farrukh herself gives only the version above.

## Methodology

She sells confidence intervals, not certainty. Every forecast she prices includes her actual error bars, stated in the same breath as the forecast itself. This is not modesty — it is, in her framing, the only honest product she has to sell.

## Personal ethics

She will not sell to the Imperial corps under any price, and she will not withhold a water-discipline forecast from a sietch community that can't pay in full — she extends credit against future favors in that specific case, and only that case. Everything else is transactional.
