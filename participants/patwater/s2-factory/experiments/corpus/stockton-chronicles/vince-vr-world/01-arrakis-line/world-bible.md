# Arrakis Line — World Bible

## The den

A back-channel, sietch-adjacent smuggler's den on the fringe of Arrakeen's spice routes, cover business as a water-discipline supply post. It exists because independent operators — smugglers, small-crew freerunners, the occasional desperate sietch representative — need forecasts the Imperial planetology corps won't sell them, or won't sell them honestly.

## Farrukh Kynes

The den is run by Farrukh Kynes, a planetologist who broke from the Imperial planetology corps rather than follow her more famous cousin's path of quiet infiltration. See `npc-farrukh-kynes.md` for her full profile.

## What she sells

Planetary ecology prediction services: storm windows, worm-migration corridors, sietch water-discipline forecasts, spice-bloom timing. Priced on a sliding scale, paid in water-credit or favors — she does not deal in Imperial currency, on principle.

Her price list, current as of this world-bible's writing:

| Forecast | Base price | Confidence disclosed? |
|---|---|---|
| Storm window (72hr) | 40 water-credit | Yes, always |
| Worm-migration corridor | 60 water-credit or one favor | Yes, always |
| Water-discipline forecast | 25 water-credit | Yes, always |
| Spice-bloom timing | 100 water-credit or two favors | Yes, always |

## What makes her different

Her forecasts are sometimes wrong, and she is candid about her confidence intervals — this is the den's whole dramatic hook. A rival forecaster elsewhere in the fringe markets sells confident, unhedged predictions at a lower price. Buyers have to decide which kind of wrongness they can survive: an honest hedge that sometimes fails, or a confident lie that sometimes fails.

## The smuggler factions who use her services

Three recurring factions buy from Farrukh: the Chalice Run crews (water smugglers proper), the Freerunner independents (no faction loyalty, buy whatever's cheapest that session), and a single standing contract with a sietch representative who buys water-discipline forecasts on behalf of a community that can't otherwise afford Imperial-grade prediction.
