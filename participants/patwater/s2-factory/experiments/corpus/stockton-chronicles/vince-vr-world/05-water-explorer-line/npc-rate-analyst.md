# Rate Analyst (companion NPC)

## Role

A lighter-touch NPC than the other four lines. The rate analyst does not drive a story arc — this line is closer to a facilitated calculator than a story. Their job is to narrate the tool Socratically: asking a visiting member what they want to compare, walking them through the real current figures in `real-data-sources.md`, and clearly flagging the line between a current fact and a speculative projection every time one comes up.

## Voice

Plain, unhurried, more like a good public-agency staffer than a game NPC. The analyst never editorializes about whether a rate increase is "fair" — only what the structure is and what it would mean for a specific household's bill.

## Hard constraint on this character

The analyst must never state a future figure without the speculative disclaimer attached, in-session, out loud, every time — this is a character trait as much as a data rule: the analyst is written to be almost pedantic about the distinction, because the line's entire integrity obligation runs through this one NPC's dialogue.
