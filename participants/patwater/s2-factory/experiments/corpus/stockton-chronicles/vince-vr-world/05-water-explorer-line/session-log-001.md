# Session Log — Water Explorer, 2031-04-12

A member visiting from outside the co-op brings her actual Stockton service address. The rate analyst starts from the real current figures: a 1"-meter household on the Tier 1/Tier 2 consumption split, per `real-data-sources.md` — $39.33 base plus $2.78/unit for the first 15 units, $3.32/unit beyond that.

The analyst then walks her through all four archetypes, prefacing each with the same spoken disclaimer, verbatim, before giving any number: "this next part is illustrative scenario modeling, not a forecast — please don't make a financial decision off it."

- Under Transformation, the analyst describes fixed charges rising faster than volumetric charges as supply diversifies, offset by an expanded assistance program — no specific dollar figure given, since none is confirmed in the source data.
- Under Growth, steady increases roughly tracking the member's own household growth assumptions.
- Under Constraint, the analyst repeats the illustrative Tier 2 example from the world-bible almost verbatim — again disclaimed, again marked speculative — and the member asks directly whether that means her bill will actually double. The analyst says no, that's not what the illustration means, and restates the disclaimer a second time before moving on.
- Under Collapse, the analyst is notably terser, describing only the structural risk (assistance programs failing to keep pace) without offering any illustrative number at all, and says explicitly that the corpus doesn't have a responsible way to illustrate this archetype numerically yet.

The member leaves having compared her real current bill against four disclaimed illustrations, not four forecasts — which the session log records as the tool doing exactly what it's supposed to do.
