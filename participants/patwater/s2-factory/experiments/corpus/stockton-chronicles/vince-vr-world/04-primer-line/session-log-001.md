# Session Log — Primer, 2031-04-10 (Ligaya and Iris)

Iris opens the session by asking, unprompted, whether the map-keeper "gets tired of walking." Ligaya, in character, doesn't answer directly — she has the map-keeper stop at a fork in the story and ask Iris which of two paths looks more tired to walk. Iris picks the rockier one "because the smooth one is probably a trick."

The map-keeper takes the rocky path and finds a small, dry riverbed. Iris asks where the water went. Ligaya again doesn't answer — she has the map-keeper say only that "it went somewhere it was needed more," and asks Iris if she thinks that's a good reason for a river to move.

Iris says yes, then adds, unprompted, "unless the place it went doesn't know how to take care of it." Ligaya logs this afterward as a possible echo of the "coming back" question noted in Iris's continuity file, without pushing on it further in-session — consistent with the line's rule that the Primer offers a story or a question back, never a direct answer to the thing a child hasn't asked outright.

The session ends without resolving where the map-keeper is going. The continuity notes are updated: the dry riverbed and "somewhere it was needed more" are now live threads for the next session.
