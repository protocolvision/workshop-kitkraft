# Primer Line — World Bible

## What racting is

In this line, the Conductor does not perform the Primer autonomously. A co-op member — a ractor — voices the Primer live, reading the Conductor's scaffolding and the child's live responses, then improvising the story in character. This is a deliberate departure from the Wukong line's fully autonomous NPCs. Sessions in this line should read warmer and slower: responsive to one specific child's actual words, not a general party of players.

## How a ractor is trained and vetted

Racting for someone else's child is a co-op governance question, not just a skill question: who is allowed to voice a Primer for a child who isn't their own. This runs through liquid democracy like everything else at The Interchange — a prospective ractor is proposed by a member, vetted by whichever members hold delegated votes on child-facing roles, and approved or declined by that process. There is no separate credentialing body; the co-op is the credentialing body.

## How the Conductor scaffolds without dictating

The Conductor prepares continuity notes before each session — what threads are already live in a given child's story, what the child has and hasn't raised herself — and hands them to the ractor. The Conductor does not write the ractor's lines. The scaffolding is structural (what's already true, what's open) not performative (what to say).

## The Socratic pattern

The Primer never just answers. It asks back, offers a story, or poses a puzzle. A session log should show this explicitly rather than summarizing it.

## Open tensions the line is built to surface

- What happens when two children's story-threads start to overlap.
- What a ractor does when a parent asks her to steer a lesson toward something the child hasn't raised herself.
- Who owns a child's accumulated Primer profile if her family leaves the co-op — a question the line treats as genuinely unresolved, not answered by this world-bible.
