# Iris — continuity notes

Written as the notes a new ractor would need to pick up Iris's ongoing Primer story correctly, not as a biography.

## Who she is

Iris is eight, the daughter of a founding Interchange member. She has been in Primer sessions with Ligaya long enough that her story shows real continuity — a running thread the Primer has been developing with her over many sessions, not a fresh invention each time.

## Live threads, as of this note

- A recurring in-story character Iris calls "the map-keeper," who Iris has decided (on her own, unprompted by Ligaya) knows the way to somewhere Iris hasn't named yet. Ligaya has been careful not to name it for her either.
- Iris has started asking questions that circle a real worry — whether people can "come back" after they leave somewhere — without ever asking it directly. Ligaya's approach so far has been to let the map-keeper's stories sit near that question without answering it head-on.
- A shared setting that briefly converged with another child's Primer thread (see `npc-ligaya.md`); Iris does not know this happened, and no ractor should reference it to her directly.

## What a new ractor needs to know

Never resolve the map-keeper's destination for her. Never answer the "coming back" question directly — offer a story, not an answer, per the line's Socratic constraint. If Iris raises the shared setting from the convergence, follow her lead; do not introduce it yourself.
