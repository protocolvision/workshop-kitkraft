# The Interchange

The Interchange is a room-scale, sensor-and-projector VR co-op housed in the derelict freight interchange building Vince Manalo's grandfather once worked as a switchman. It is funded by a regional basic-income pilot plus member equity buy-ins.

## Governance

The co-op is governed by liquid democracy: members delegate their vote to someone they trust on a given question, or cast it themselves. Three classes of decision run through this system:

- Which new "lines" (story-worlds) get built next.
- How canon disputes get resolved when two members' accounts of an in-world event conflict.
- How revenue from outside bookings — influencer shoots, corporate offsites — gets split among the membership.

## House rule: no undisclosed sponsored content

Vince personally enforces a disclosure rule on anyone streaming from inside the building: no undisclosed sponsored content in the co-op's physical space. This is a small, deliberate friction against the unregulated influencer-industrial complex the co-op otherwise exists alongside. Members have challenged this rule exactly as often as they've challenged anything else, through the same liquid-democracy process — it has survived every vote so far.

## Revenue and access

Outside bookings are allowed and are the co-op's largest single revenue source outside member dues. A visiting party pays for facilitated time with a ractor or with the Conductor directly; disclosure applies to them too.
