# The Conductor

The Conductor is The Interchange's always-on AI agent, delivered through members' smart glasses. It narrates sessions, improvises NPC dialogue, and answers player questions.

## What makes it different from a general AI agent

The Conductor is explicitly a retrieval-augmented system. It draws only on a bounded lore corpus that Vince and the membership have written and voted into canon. It is not supposed to invent facts outside that corpus, and any member can challenge it mid-session to cite its source for a claim. A challenge that returns no verified source is treated as a Conductor error, logged, and reviewed at the next governance session.

## Accountability

The Conductor is accountable to the co-op — wearer-side — not to an outside vendor. This is a deliberate governance choice: elsewhere, the same always-on-agent-in-smart-glasses pattern is deployed employer-side, answering to whoever pays for the deployment rather than whoever wears the glasses. The Interchange's members hold that distinction to be the whole point of building their own version rather than licensing someone else's.

## Relationship to the five lines

The Conductor routes a session down one of five prebuilt "lines" at the party's request, or improvises a session that stays on the baseline Stockton Chronicles frame story. Four of the five lines are explicitly simulations members knowingly step into. The fifth — the Water Explorer line — is treated differently by the Conductor: it draws on a separate, more strictly sourced corpus and never lets a scenario projection be mistaken for a forecast.
