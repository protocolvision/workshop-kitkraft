# The Seam

Vince's grandfather used to tell him that on certain nights, the interchange yard received trains that weren't on any timetable. This is old Delta rail folklore, and inside The Interchange it has a name: the Seam. The building is said to sit on it.

The Seam is the diegetic justification for the range of story-worlds The Interchange's Conductor can route a session down. It is consistent with, and an extension of, the wider Trainverse canon in which Stockton functions as a liminal crossing point between story-lines.

## The five lines

The Conductor can route a session down one of five prebuilt lines:

1. Arrakis — a smuggler's den on the fringe of the spice routes.
2. The Wukong Line — the Monkey King loose in contemporary California.
3. The knowledge firm from the Golden Age of Piracy.
4. The Primer Line — a live-ractor storytelling companion for a specific child.
5. The Water Explorer — a civic scenario-modeling tool, not a game.

The Stockton Chronicles baseline reality stays grounded and near-future throughout. Genre content in the first four lines is explicitly a simulation members knowingly step into — the same way a holodeck episode doesn't break a show's own setting. The fifth line is the deliberate exception: it is not fiction members step out of at the end of a session, and its Conductor corpus is held to a stricter sourcing standard than the other four.
