# Knowledge Firm Line — World Bible

## Founding

Founded circa the early 1700s by a coalition of former privateers, quartermasters, and navigators who realized that verified intelligence — safe anchorages, naval patrol patterns, cargo manifests, weather windows — was worth more, and far safer to sell, than plunder itself.

## The founding articles

Modeled directly on pirate ship articles: elected leadership, term limits, disclosed shares, and a formal dispute process. This is an early liquid-democracy analog, and worth naming explicitly as such — it is the direct thematic ancestor of The Interchange's own governance model, centuries later.

## Survival strategy

Over centuries the firm survived by becoming ever more selective and discreet: fewer clients, higher trust, verification standards that got stricter rather than looser as the value of bad intelligence to a client (financial or otherwise) grew.

## Present day

By the Stockton Chronicles' near-future, the firm operates as a quiet information-brokerage with an unmarked Stockton office. It occasionally supplies tips to Vince's co-op — some of the setting's cross-line NPCs originate as firm contacts — or appears as an NPC faction across other lines.

## What it trades in now

Verified intelligence of the modern equivalent: supply-chain timing, regulatory-filing patterns, which claims in a public dispute are actually documented versus asserted. The firm's disclosure-and-verification code is treated in-corpus as directly analogous to the citation-required discipline the Conductor itself follows — a session in this line can make that parallel explicit.
