# Session Log — Knowledge Firm, 2031-04-08

The party, working a job through the firm's unmarked Stockton office, is offered a shortcut by an outside contact: skip the firm's standard verification pass on a supply-chain rumor and act on it immediately, before a rival buyer does. The firm's own local handler — invoking "Quire's Rule" the way the world-bible describes, as present-day shorthand rather than a literal historical quote — refuses to sign off without verification, even though it costs the party the time advantage.

The table split: two players wanted to take the outside contact's shortcut and eat the firm's disapproval; one argued the firm's whole value proposition collapses the moment it starts selling unverified claims, and that the rival buyer acting on bad information isn't actually a loss for their side. They ultimately waited for verification. The rumor turned out to be half true — the supply timing was accurate, the volume figure was inflated by the outside contact for reasons the session left unresolved.
