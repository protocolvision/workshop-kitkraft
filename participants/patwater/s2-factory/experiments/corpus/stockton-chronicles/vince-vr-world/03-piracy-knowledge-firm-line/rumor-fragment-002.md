# A less flattering version of "Quire's Rule"

"You know the story where Quire just decided disclosed shares was the right thing to do? I heard it different — I heard the crew threatened to maroon him over a pay dispute and disclosed shares was the deal he cut to save his own neck, not some principled stand. Convenient that the version everyone tells now makes him look like a visionary instead of a man bargaining for his life."

This directly contradicts the Tier-2 NPC profile (`npc-josiah-quire.md`), which credits Quire with insisting on the disclosed-shares provision by firm tradition. Deliberately included as this line's required Tier-2 contradiction; the world-bible itself notes no surviving document more specific than the articles confirms either version.
