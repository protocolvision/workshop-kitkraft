# Josiah Quire

## Founding role

Quire was quartermaster aboard one of the ships whose crew founded the firm, and is credited — by firm tradition, not by any surviving document more specific than the articles themselves — with insisting that the founding articles include disclosed shares. His argument, as the tradition has it: a crew that can see exactly what everyone else is paid has one fewer reason to mutiny over money, which was, in a knowledge-brokering trade, the only kind of mutiny that mattered anymore.

## How he's used in the present day

Quire is referenced, not portrayed. Present-day firm members invoke him the way a modern institution invokes a founding figure whose actual voice nobody alive has heard: "Quire's Rule" is present-day shorthand for the disclosure-and-verification standard, applied to a new situation the historical Quire never encountered.

## Session use

A session in this line can treat "what would Quire have done" as a genuine in-fiction argument between NPCs, not a settled answer — the firm's own members disagree about how literally to apply a 300-year-old standard to a modern deal.
