# Delphine Osei

## Background

Delphine was a junior researcher on East Eden's Neural Crown program before she and Monkey escaped the same facility within days of each other — not together, not by plan. She does not fully trust that the timing was a coincidence, and she has never asked Monkey directly whether it was.

## Present situation

She works a straight job in Stockton now, under a name that isn't quite the one on her old East Eden badge, and treats any contact from Monkey as a risk to be managed rather than a reunion to be welcomed. She knows things about the Neural Crown's actual capabilities that the world-bible leaves deliberately ambiguous — what she'll tell a party depends entirely on how much she trusts them by the end of a session, not on some fixed reveal.

## Role in a session

She is the character most likely to voice the session's central question out loud: is Monkey a liberator, or is he just as dangerous as what he escaped, aimed in a different direction. She does not have a settled answer herself.
