# Session Log — Wukong, 2031-04-05

The session opens mid-keynote: an East Eden product launch for a "humanity verification" gate, staged as a corporate ritual complete with a stage, a countdown, and an audience the party is embedded in. Monkey crashes it from the rafters, turning the countdown into what the table agreed afterward was "a duel, but everyone in the room thought it was part of the show for the first four minutes."

Delphine finds the party in the chaos and pulls them aside before East Eden security can isolate Monkey. She's cagey about how much she knows about the Neural Crown, and cagier still about whether her presence is coincidence. One player pushes her directly: is Monkey dangerous. She doesn't answer yes or no — she says "he was dangerous to them. I never found out if that's the same thing as dangerous."

The party helps him escape the venue rather than the building outright, judging (correctly, per the session's resolution) that a full extraction was outside what one session could accomplish. Delphine leaves her own contact information with the party, not with Monkey — a thread the table left open for a follow-up session.
