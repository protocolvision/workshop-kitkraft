# Wukong Line — World Bible

This is an in-corpus lore digest of the relevant West of the West canon, written for the Conductor's retrieval corpus rather than reproducing any original prose.

## Flower Fruit Mountain

A hydroelectric co-op near Big Sur, run collectively by a community that includes Monkey among its founding members. It is the closest thing Monkey has to a home base and the place a party can go to find allies who already know him.

## East Eden Industries

A recurring corporate antagonist: a fictionalized composite tech conglomerate, not any real company. East Eden runs the brain-computer-interface research program Monkey escaped from, and is generally positioned as the setting's face of unaccountable, platform-scale technological power.

## The Neural Crown

A BCI device East Eden's research program was developing, of which Monkey was a test subject ("Subject M-80" in lab records — see below). The Crown's exact capabilities are deliberately left ambiguous in this world-bible; individual session logs and NPC accounts may describe it differently, which is intentional line texture rather than an error.

## TIMSHEL

The recurring motto of the line: "Thou mayest" — the idea that a choice remains possible even inside a determined-feeling system. Monkey invokes it, mockingly or sincerely depending on the session, whenever a party is deciding whether to help him or stop him.

## Subject M-80

Monkey's designation inside East Eden's BCI program before his escape. What actually happened during the escape is contested — see the line's rumor fragments for the specific contradictions a session can surface.

## Tone

Slapstick, irreverent, fast. Willing to turn a tech-industry ritual — a "humanity verification" gate, a keynote, a term-sheet negotiation — into a mythic duel without breaking the baseline Stockton Chronicles frame that the whole thing is a VR simulation members knowingly stepped into.
