## Kits I use
### 1. Reel machine
- What it is: A Claude Code skill plus scripts for animation and audio, a launchd job, and CapCut. It takes a talking-head take I filmed and builds the project I open.
- The job it does: Turns a raw take I filmed into a CapCut project with cuts, captions and pacing, ready for me to finish and export.
- How often I use it: Daily
- What I adapted: The deliverable is a CapCut project I open and modify, never a finished MP4. I keep the last edit and the export. I also moved to one folder per reel so the media relinks instead of breaking.
- Who else uses it: Only me
- Where it lives: The AI Motor workspace on my machine, as a skill plus an automations folder, with one folder per reel.

### 2. The Garage (content machine) with content-brief
- What it is: A pipeline that pulls posts, embeds and clusters them into idea clusters in Supabase, a web view of those clusters, and a skill that turns a cluster into a brief.
- The job it does: Turns the noise of other people's content into clusters of ideas and then into a brief I can actually film or design.
- How often I use it: Weekly
- What I adapted: I tuned the clustering thresholds by hand until the clusters stopped being duplicates, added a dedupe pass, and made the brief come out in my own design system and voice instead of generic output.
- Who else uses it: Only me
- Where it lives: The AI Motor workspace, as an automations folder plus a skill, with the data in my own Supabase project.

### 3. Bitacora (the journal system)
- What it is: A contract written into my workspace rules plus a plugin: MOCs as topic hubs, one file per session, daily notes, frozen decisions, and living logbooks per topic, with link-back rules the agent has to follow.
- The job it does: Makes every working session with Claude leave a trace the next session can pick up, so I never explain my own context twice.
- How often I use it: Daily. It runs on every session; there are around 800 session files across my two workspaces.
- What I adapted: I fixed the seven document types and the link-back rule myself after losing work, made the daily notes append-only because several sessions run at once, and packaged the whole thing as a plugin so it travels to other workspaces.
- Who else uses it: Only me
- Where it lives: In my workspace rules and journal folders, with the canonical source as a plugin in The AI Motor workspace.
