## Email

Hi everyone,

Thank you for being part of AI Kitcraft on 21 and 22 September. And if you signed up
but couldn't make it, thank you too. This was the first time we'd run the workshop, and
we learned a lot from everyone who came.

Over the two days, most people had their AI assistant take stock of the tools they
already use, then read each other's lists looking for work that fits with their own.
By the end, seven people had started building what we called a factory: a tool that
runs without its author. A few had begun connecting their work to someone else's. That
second part is really what the workshop is about, learning to work together when
everyone's AI is set up a little differently.

**Everything is in one place**

The slides, a written summary of each session, and everyone's inventories, factories
and bridges are here:
https://github.com/protocolvision/workshop-kitkraft

Your folder stays open, and you're welcome to keep adding to it.

One note on the recordings. We've published written summaries of the sessions rather
than the transcripts, and they don't quote or name anyone who took part:

email and we'll remove it. No reason needed.
