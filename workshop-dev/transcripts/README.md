# Session records — AI Kitcraft, September 2026

Synthesized summaries only. **The raw transcripts and the auto-generated per-session
notes are deliberately not in this repo, and should not be added to it.**

## Why these are summaries and not transcripts

This repository is public. The recordings captured sixteen people talking casually,
at length, about their work, their employers and their own practice. A verbal notice
at the top of session one is a reasonable basis for taking a recording; it is a thin
basis for publishing every sentence someone said, under their name, permanently and
indexed, on the open web.

So each session here is a written synthesis: detailed on substance, silent on
everything else. The ideas, the arguments, the disagreements and the references are
preserved, because those are what the workshop was for and what a future run needs.
The verbatim record is not.

## The rule applied to every file

- **No person-specific quotes.** Nothing is reproduced verbatim and attributed to an
  individual. Ideas are attributed; sentences are not.
- **No personal disclosures.** Financial matters, family, health, employment
  insecurity and anything else a person mentioned in passing about their own life is
  out, whether or not it was interesting.
- **No employer-internal detail.** Where someone described how their organisation
  actually works, the general point is kept and the specifics are not.
- **No registration or attendance administration.** Who signed up, who did not, and
  what was predicted about turnout is not recorded here.
- **Private planning calls are included, but held to the same rule**, and more
  strictly: the two facilitator calls were not announced to anyone and the people
  discussed in them were not present.

## Accuracy

The source recordings were auto-transcribed from a voice channel with several people
talking at once. Long passages are interleaved to the point where two speakers'
sentences alternate line by line, and the automatic speaker attribution is wrong in
places. These summaries were written with that in mind: where attribution was
uncertain, the contribution is recorded without a name rather than guessed at.

Treat any attribution here as good-faith reconstruction, not as a quotation record.

## If you were in the room

If anything here misrepresents you, or you would rather not be named at all, say so
and it comes out. No justification needed and no discussion required. That was the
promise made when the sessions were recorded, and it does not expire.
